Cricket Rules Overview

Basic Rules

  • Two teams of 11 players each take turns batting and fielding
  • Matches are divided into innings where each team bats once
  • The batting team scores runs by hitting the ball and running between wickets
  • The fielding team attempts to get batsmen out through various methods
  • The team with the most runs wins

Player Roles

  • Batsmen: Score runs by hitting the ball and running between wickets
  • Bowlers: Deliver the ball to batsmen, attempting to dismiss them
  • Wicket-keeper: Stands behind stumps to catch balls and stump batsmen
  • Fielders: Prevent runs and attempt to get batsmen out
  • All-rounders: Skilled at both batting and bowling

Ways to Score

  • Running between wickets: 1 run per completed run
  • Boundary four: Ball reaches boundary after bouncing - 4 runs
  • Boundary six: Ball clears boundary without bouncing - 6 runs
  • Byes: Runs scored when batsman misses ball
  • Leg byes: Runs scored when ball hits batsman's body

Ways to Get Out

  • Bowled: Bowler hits stumps with ball
  • Caught: Fielder catches hit ball before it bounces
  • LBW: Ball hits batsman's leg in line with stumps
  • Run out: Fielder hits stumps while batsman is running
  • Stumped: Wicket-keeper removes bails while batsman is out of crease
